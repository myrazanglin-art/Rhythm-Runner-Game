Rhythm Runner

Multi-Layer Audio Analysis. Automated Beatmap Generation.

Project Overview

A 2D rhythm game that eliminates manual charting. A fully automated pipeline driven by multi-layer audio analysis translates arbitrary audio files into playable levels.

一款抛弃了人工打谱的 2D 音乐节奏游戏。由多层音频分析驱动的全自动流水线，将任意音频转化为可玩的游戏关卡。

Tailored for complex genres including experimental electronic, post-rock, and indie music, the system generates dynamic Easy, Normal, and Hard charts in real time.

针对实验电子、后摇和独立音乐等复杂流派，系统能够实时生成动态的 Easy、Normal 和 Hard 谱面。

Computer Music & DSP

Rather than relying on monotonous BPM tracking, 15+ complementary descriptors are extracted to decode the soul of the track:

抛弃了单调的 BPM 节拍追踪，通过提取 15 种以上的互补描述符来解构音轨的灵魂：

  • Spectral & Temporal Analysis Extracts onset strengths, spectral flux, and zero-crossing rate to capture density and chaotic energy.
  • Energy & Dynamics Maps RMS energy to dynamically scale obstacle density, ensuring quiet ambient sections are sparse while drops are intense.
  • HPSS & Melodic Contours Utilizes Harmonic-Percussive Source Separation and piptrack to detect sustained holds and melodic gestures.
