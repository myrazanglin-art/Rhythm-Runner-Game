Rhythm Runner

PROJECT_OVERVIEW

Rhythm Runner is a 2D rhythm game that abandons manual charting. It features a fully automated pipeline driven by multi-layer audio analysis, translating arbitrary audio files into playable levels.

Rhythm Runner 是一款抛弃了人工打谱的 2D 音乐节奏游戏。它由多层音频分析驱动，全自动地将任意音频转化为可玩的游戏关卡。

Built for complex genres like experimental electronic, post-rock, and indie music, the system generates dynamic Easy, Normal, and Hard charts in real time.

针对实验电子、后摇和独立音乐等复杂流派，系统能够实时生成动态的 Easy、Normal 和 Hard 谱面。

COMPUTER_MUSIC_&_DSP

Instead of relying on monotonous BPM tracking, we extract 15+ complementary descriptors to understand the soul of the track.

我们没有依赖单调的 BPM 节拍追踪，而是提取了 15 种以上的互补描述符来理解音轨的灵魂：

librosa.feature.spectral_centroid()
librosa.feature.spectral_rolloff()
librosa.feature.spectral_flatness()
librosa.feature.zero_crossing_rate()
  • Spectral & Temporal: Extracts onset strengths, spectral flux, and zero-crossing rate to capture the density and chaotic energy of the audio.
  • Energy & Dynamics: Maps RMS energy and dynamic range to dynamically scale the density of obstacles and enemies in sections like choruses or ambient breakdowns.
  • HPSS & Melodic Contours: Utilizes Harmonic-Percussive Source Separation (HPSS) and piptrack to detect sustained holds and melodic gestures, mapping them into specific note types.
  • Structural Segmentation: Uses CQT chroma and recurrence segmentation to identify musical sections, driving the dynamic difficulty adjustments.

ENGINE_ARCHITECTURE

  • Stage 1: Python Offline Analysis
    Extracts multi-dimensional features and serializes them into a JSON beatmap, assigning notes to dual lanes based on frequency band dominance.
  • Stage 2: Godot Real-time Execution
    Ingests the JSON data, spawning entities early to ensure perfect travel time. Features a distance-based judgment system that scales with difficulty for precise rhythmic feedback.
